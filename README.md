# Rooms

A RimWorld 1.6 mod inspired by liminal spaces. Adds a new planet layer — the Rooms — with its own biomes, factions, incidents, and survival loop.

## Entering the Rooms

Find or build a portal, or wait for the passageway to open.

## Features

- **Three biomes to explore**: endless carpeted corridors, ceramic-tiled Pool Rooms with fishable water, and open concrete Parking levels.
- **4 factions that inhabit the Rooms layer**: Researchers in hazmat suits who will try to kidnap your downed pawns, hostile survivors who will raid your settlements for resources, Spawns of the yellow rooms that thirst for destruction, and neutral survivors who may offer to trade.
- **Survive and farm**: grow edible and medicinal mushrooms in the dark on carpet plots, fish in the pools for food and rare catches, craft rubber from chemfuel, sew hazmat suits and helmets, smelt broken lamps back into steel.

## Requirements

- RimWorld 1.6
- [Harmony](https://github.com/pardeike/HarmonyRimWorld) (brrainz.harmony)

## Installation

**Steam Workshop** — subscribe to the mod (recommended).

**Manual** — download/clone this repository into your `Mods` folder and enable *Rooms* in the mod list.
